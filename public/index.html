Maintenance windows

A window withholds notification for a named set until it ends. The alerts still raise, stay on every page and the wall marked "in maintenance", and anything still active when the window closes is delivered on the next pass. The watchdog is never suppressed.

Thresholds

The shipped defaults are a starting point. An override replaces them for one sensor (by its code), for one kind on one device, or for that kind everywhere. A muted override suspends the rule without touching polling or history. Narrower always wins: sensor beats device beats kind beats default. Set overrides from a sensor's card on its device page; this is the fleet-wide view.

Syslog and trap alert rules

Raise an alert when an incoming syslog message or SNMP trap matches a pattern - "authentication failure" in a log line, or a linkDown trap's OID. The alert raises the moment a matching message arrives, names the device that sent it, and clears on its own once no matching message has arrived for the "clears after" time. Rules run on every message received, so keep patterns specific.

Group alerts

One alert for a location or application when enough of its devices are down at once, instead of an email for each. Tick a group to watch it. It trips when at least the percent shown of its devices are down - counting only devices whose status is known - and at least the minimum number, so a small group cannot trip on one failure. While the group alert is open, its devices' own alerts still raise and show, but their emails are held: the group's email names them. Any still down when the group recovers are emailed then. Transient and muted devices are not counted.

Credentials

A profile is a named SNMP credential the collector sends to every device that names it. Use read-only communities and users: if this store is ever compromised, the attacker should be able to read your devices, not change them. Secrets are encrypted at rest and never shown again once saved.
